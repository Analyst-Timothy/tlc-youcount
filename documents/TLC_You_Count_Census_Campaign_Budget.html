5. Cost controls

  • Fund Twilio progressively rather than budgeting against all 18,000 database records.
  • Set low-balance and unusual-spend alerts before campaign launch.
  • Retain per-session, phone and identity security limits while allowing shared church Wi-Fi traffic.
  • Monitor completion by location and service after each Sunday.
  • Send the TLC ID email once per identity, rather than after every subsequent edit.
  • Keep photos compressed and apply the agreed 90-day retention policy to relevant campaign and audit data.

6. Recommendation

Approve £650 for the initial You Count census campaign. This supports a realistic reach of up to approximately 4,000 completed profiles under the stated country mix. Authorise an additional £150 contingency only if participation approaches 5,000 or the proportion of Nigerian SMS destinations is materially higher than assumed.
