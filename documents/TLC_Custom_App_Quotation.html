1. Executive summary

This quotation covers the design, development, integration and production launch of a custom TLC digital member platform. The platform will provide a unified TLC-branded experience across iOS, Android, web and administration while retaining Planning Center as the official source for selected church records.

The platform will combine member identity, personalised dashboards, discipleship journeys, content, serving, groups, targeted communication and engagement intelligence in one TLC-owned product.

2. Project scope

Member experience

  • TLC-branded iOS and Android mobile application.
  • Responsive member web experience.
  • Secure member registration, sign-in and account recovery.
  • TLC ID display and member profile management.
  • Personalised home dashboard based on member journey and activity.
  • Today’s message, sermons, devotions, announcements and featured content.
  • Events, groups, serving teams and relevant member actions.
  • Bookmarks, recent activity and notification inbox.
  • Push, email and SMS communication support.

Discipleship and engagement intelligence

  • Configurable Connect, Master Life, Serve and other TLC journey stages.
  • Visible next steps and milestone indicators.
  • Rule-based personalised dashboard cards.
  • Dynamic member audiences based on approved church data and engagement.
  • Targeted in-app, email, push and SMS campaigns.
  • Campaign scheduling and basic automated journeys.
  • Engagement, movement and conversion reporting.
  • Member-care and follow-up queues for authorised staff.

Teams and church participation

  • Serving team discovery and join requests.
  • Volunteer onboarding and approval.
  • Team membership and role visibility.
  • Rota, availability and replacement workflows where supported by the agreed Planning Center data.
  • Team guides, training content and document access.

Administration and integration

  • Secure web-based administration portal.
  • Role-based permissions for administrators, ministry leaders and content managers.
  • Content, journey, audience and campaign management.
  • Planning Center synchronisation for agreed people, groups, teams and journey fields.
  • Audit history, integration health monitoring and failure alerts.
  • Production deployment and submission to Apple App Store and Google Play.
  • Technical handover and administrator orientation.

5. Maintenance and support

Monthly maintenance fee: £300

A 90-day defect warranty is included from production launch. Monthly maintenance commences after the warranty period unless TLC requests an earlier managed-support start.

The maintenance service includes:

  • Production availability and integration monitoring.
  • Security and dependency updates.
  • Planning Center integration compatibility maintenance.
  • Routine database and scheduled-job health checks.
  • App Store and operating-system compatibility updates.
  • Correction of reproducible defects in the delivered scope.
  • Reasonable email-based technical support and a monthly health summary.

Maintenance excludes new modules, major feature changes, redesigns, new third-party integrations, bulk content entry, large data migrations and incidents caused by unauthorised changes or third-party outages. Such work will be separately agreed and quoted.

7. Data architecture

Planning Center will remain the official source for agreed church records. A separate TLC operational database is required for app accounts, device sessions, TLC IDs, content, journeys, audiences, engagement events, notifications, permissions and audit history. Only the minimum information required for the agreed purposes will be synchronised.

This design avoids duplicating Planning Center unnecessarily while providing the performance, resilience and intelligence required by the custom application.

8. Ownership and handover

  • TLC owns all TLC member data, content and church records at all times.
  • Following final payment, TLC will own the completed TLC-specific application source code, project-specific designs, database structures, configuration and documentation.
  • Pre-existing generic utilities, frameworks and reusable development components remain the developer’s property, with TLC receiving a perpetual licence to use them as incorporated into the platform.
  • TLC will own the production hosting, database, messaging and app-store accounts.
  • Source-code and operational handover will be completed after final payment.

9. Scope boundaries and assumptions

  • The quotation covers the agreed first production release and its defined engagement-intelligence capabilities.
  • Replacement of Planning Center’s giving, registrations, check-in or accounting products is not included.
  • Advanced AI classification, donor-health modelling, additional external systems and bespoke hardware integrations are excluded unless separately agreed.
  • TLC will supply approved content, privacy wording, journey definitions, administrator decisions and required account access.
  • Material scope changes may affect cost and delivery dates and will require written approval.
  • Supplier costs, taxes, VAT where applicable, exchange-rate changes and messaging charges are excluded from the £10,000 development fee.
